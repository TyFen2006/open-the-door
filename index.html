The Pause

Nerves make you fill every silence. Pros let it breathe. Read the line out loud, hit the last word, then hold the silence until the ring closes.

3 second hold

"…and that's exactly why teams switch to us."

Why this worksA 3-second pause feels like forever to you and like confidence to them. It says "I'm not rushing to convince you — I know this is good."

Tonality reps

Same words, different intention. Read each line out loud in the tone it asks for. Tap play to hear a model pace, then say it back.

Active listening

Great sellers reflect before they respond. Play the buyer, then answer out loud — start by naming what you heard ("So what I'm hearing is…") before you pitch.

The skeptic

Tap play to hear the buyer.

The 3-step reflect1) Name it: "So it sounds like…" 2) Validate: "That's a totally fair concern." 3) Then bridge to your point. Do this and they feel heard — that's what makes a connection real, not surface-level.
